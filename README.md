## The scene

> Every Sunday, Dad inspects Mum's roast like a general, carves one slice,
> nods once, and says, 'Not bad' — which in our house means he loved it so
> much he went back for thirds.

One line, 175 characters: a small Sunday-roast ritual, a father's understatement
as a love language, and a quiet turn — "Not bad" means he went back for thirds.

## How it works

`main.py` (standard library only) validates the scene against the competition
gate (1–280 characters, one or two lines, no control characters) and writes it
to `/output/entry.txt`.
